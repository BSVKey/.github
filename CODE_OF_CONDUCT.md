# Code of Conduct

This applies to every BSVKey repository, issue, pull request and discussion.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and constructive.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, scams, or promotion of unrelated tokens or projects.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block
accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately, email
**embryospace@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
